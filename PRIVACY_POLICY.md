# Privacy Policy

**S. Mahi Global Services Ltd — S-MAHII**
Effective date: July 25, 2026

S. Mahi Global Services Ltd ("S-MAHII," "we," "us," or "our") operates the S-MAHII mobile application and website (together, the "Service"), a digital marketplace connecting identity-verified artisans and service providers with clients across Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and profile information
When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, password (stored as a secure one-way hash — we never see or store your plaintext password), role (client, artisan, agent, coordinator), and address/location details (country, state, local government area). Artisans additionally provide a profession/category, years of experience, hourly rate, and a short bio.

### 1.2 Identity verification documents
Artisans and service providers who apply for a verified badge submit identity/verification documents (e.g. a government-issued ID or proof of trade). These are reviewed by our agents solely to confirm identity and trade legitimacy.

### 1.3 Location
With your permission, we collect your device's GPS coordinates to show you artisans near you and to calculate distance. You can decline this permission and use the app with a manually entered location instead.

### 1.4 Photos and camera access
You may attach photos to a booking (e.g. a photo of the job to be done) or upload a profile/portfolio picture. These are uploaded to our image-hosting provider, Cloudinary (see Section 3).

### 1.5 Voice and chat content
If you use voice input to search or message, your speech is transcribed to text using a third-party AI service (OpenAI's Whisper). Messages you send to our in-app AI assistant, or to another user via chat, are processed to provide those features; AI assistant conversations are additionally sent to OpenAI to generate a response.

### 1.6 Payment information
Artisans pay a one-time registration fee via Paystack, our payment processor. We do not collect or store your card, bank account, or other payment credentials — these are entered directly into Paystack's own secure payment page and never pass through our servers. We retain only the payment reference, amount, and status.

### 1.7 Device and push notification data
If you enable notifications, we store an Expo push token identifying your device, so we can deliver booking, chat, and account alerts. We also record when your account was last active, so artisans can show an "online now" indicator to prospective clients.

### 1.8 Usage and marketplace data
We keep a record of bookings, reviews and ratings, saved/favorited artisans, dispute reports, and in-app notifications, as this is core to how the marketplace functions.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Create and manage your account and verify artisan identities
- Connect clients with nearby, relevant artisans
- Process bookings, cancellations, and reviews
- Facilitate in-app chat and our AI assistant
- Process the artisan registration fee via Paystack
- Send booking, chat, and account notifications
- Investigate disputes and enforce our terms of service
- Improve and secure the Service

We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share limited information with the following service providers, solely to operate the features described above. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and security practices:

| Provider | What we share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Paystack** | Email, payment amount | Processing the artisan registration fee |
| **Cloudinary** | Photos you upload | Hosting booking/profile/portfolio images |
| **OpenAI** | Voice recordings, chat messages sent to the AI assistant | Speech-to-text transcription and AI assistant responses |
| **Brevo** | Email address | Sending verification codes, password resets, and account emails |
| **Expo (push notification service)** | Device push token | Delivering push notifications |

We do not permit these providers to use your information for their own advertising purposes.

## 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal/accounting obligations.

You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Personal Information → Delete my account**, which requires re-entering your password to confirm. Deleting your account immediately deactivates it and revokes all of your active login sessions. Some records — such as completed booking and payment history — may be retained afterward where necessary for legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, or marketplace-integrity purposes (for example, so a review you left cannot simply be erased by the person it concerns).

To request deletion of your account, or to ask a question about your data, contact us using the details in Section 8.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information (most fields are editable directly in the app under Personal Information)
- Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to Section 4
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Withdraw consent for location, camera, microphone, or notification permissions at any time via your device settings

Nigerian users have these rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. To exercise any of them, contact us using the details in Section 8.
